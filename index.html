Robotics Simulators

Browser-based 3D simulators for industrial robot arms, with controller-style program operation. Choose an arm tab to see its photos and details, then open its simulator. The comparison below shows what the three have in common and where they differ.

Fanuc ER-4iA

Open simulator →

6-axis education arm · R-30iB Mate Plus controller

A compact 6-axis arm for hands-on training and small-part automation, with a 4 kg payload and 550 mm reach. Students jog joints J1 to J6 or the tool in X, Y and Z, and run sample or custom programs in a TP-style subset with grippers, I/O and a simulated controller.

J6FlangeTurns the tool mounting flange.±360° (simulator)

Motors and drives

Each joint is moved by its own servo motor through a gear reducer. Each motor has an encoder that reports its position to the controller, and a brake that holds the joint when servo power is off. This is typical for this class of arm; the simulator does not model the motors themselves.

Structure

  • Base column 300 mm
  • Upper arm 240 mm
  • Forearm 260 mm
  • Wrist 50 mm
  • Flange 70 mm

Notes

  • Datasheet: mechanical weight 20 kg, repeatability ±0.01 mm, 550 mm reach, 4 kg wrist payload.
  • The datasheet gives total travel of 340° (J1), 230° (J2), 402° (J3), 380° (J4), 240° (J5) and 720° (J6). The simulator ranges above are approximations and differ for some joints.
  • Protection IP50 on the body and wrist (datasheet).

Ultra-compact controller for small MOTOMAN robots · INFORM-style teaching

The YRC1000micro is a small controller in an open-structure cabinet, aimed at pick-and-place and assembly. The simulator models a generic small 6-axis arm with a teach-pendant style controller (TEACH, PLAY and REMOTE modes), a payload interlock, digital I/O, a cycle timer and an INFORM-style subset.

7-axis single-arm collaborative robot · OmniCore C30 controller

Common to all three

  • A deadman ENABLE button for any hand movement.
  • SERVO ON / OFF before any motion, and an E-STOP that latches until RESET.
  • A payload check that blocks a program run when the payload is too high.
  • Joint and Cartesian jog, and the same program controls: Run, Step, Pause, Stop, Edit.
  • A cycle timer and cycle count on the STATUS tab.

Unique to each

  • Fanuc ER-4iA: three modes (AUTO, T1, T2), the largest I/O (8 inputs and 8 outputs), and a 4 kg payload with 550 mm reach.
  • Yaskawa YRC1000micro: TEACH, PLAY and REMOTE modes, with START used in REMOTE only, and INFORM-style commands.
  • ABB IRB 14050 YuMi: seven axes, a 0.5 kg payload and 559 mm reach, a 250 mm/s TCP limit in MANUAL, and RAPID-style commands.

Sources: Fanuc values from fanucamerica.com and fanuc.eu. ABB values are from the IRB 14050 datasheet as cited in its README. The Yaskawa payload is an illustrative rating for this model, and its reach is not given.
