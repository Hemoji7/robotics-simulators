Robotics Simulators

Browser-based 3D simulators for industrial robot arms, with controller-style program operation. Each card shows the real arm, its controller and a typical setup. Click a card to open its simulator.

Available

Fanuc ER-4iA

6-axis education arm · R-30iB Mate Plus controller

A compact 6-axis arm for hands-on training and small-part automation, with a 4 kg payload and 550 mm reach. Students jog joints J1 to J6 or the tool in X, Y and Z, and run sample or custom programs in a TP-style subset with grippers, I/O and a simulated controller.

Available

Yaskawa YRC1000micro

Ultra-compact controller for small MOTOMAN robots · INFORM-style teaching

The YRC1000micro is a small controller in an open-structure cabinet, aimed at pick-and-place and assembly. The simulator models a generic small 6-axis arm with a teach-pendant style controller (TEACH, PLAY and REMOTE modes), a payload interlock, digital I/O, a cycle timer and an INFORM-style subset.

Open the Yaskawa YRC1000micro simulator
Available

ABB IRB 14050 YuMi (OmniCore C30)

7-axis single-arm collaborative robot · OmniCore C30 controller

A lightweight single-arm YuMi with a 0.5 kg payload and 559 mm reach, built for small-part assembly and testing. The simulator uses RAPID-style lines, MANUAL and AUTO modes, a servo-gripper or vacuum cup, datasheet joint limits and joint colour coding.
